*Royal Mail [Enter Solution/Application Acronym here, e.g PDA] - Processing Sites: Worked Example*

Royal Mail operates a national network of Processing Sites - Mail Centres, RDCs, Hubs and Super Hubs, International, through which all letter and parcel volumes are sorted, consolidated, and dispatched. Workforce planning across this estate has historically relied on a combination of MDS (Master Duty System), spreadsheet-based duty rosters, and locally held overtime sign-off processes. This fragmentation creates discrepancies between planned hours, scanned attendance, and paid hours in PSP, with downstream consequences for cost recognition, finance reporting, and audit assurance.

The [Enter Solution/Application Name here, e.g PDA] ([Enter Solution/Application Acronym here, e.g PDA]) application already deployed for Delivery Offices is being extended to Processing Sites to provide a single, integrated, auditable workforce-planning platform across the frontline estate. The MVP scope is defined in the Processing - High Level Requirements artefact (story IDs MDS++_++MVP01++_++01 through MDS++_++MVP24++_++01).

*Capabilities delivered by [Enter Solution/Application Acronym here, e.g PDA] at Processing Sites:*

|===
|*++#++* |*Capability* |*Description*
|1 |Daily Attendance Planning |Structured scheduling interfaces for duty planning across delivery offices and processing sites. Consumes master duty and reference data from DDS for deliveries and MDS for Processing sites , establishing a standardised, auditable record of scheduling intent across the frontline estate.
|2 |Overtime Planning |A dedicated scheduling environment for overtime attendance across delivery offices and processing sites. Generates structured OT plans that serve as the authoritative reference for Qlik Reporting.
|3 |Overtime Timesheet |Cross-references the approved OT plan against real-time PDA and Console attendance scan data. Authorised personnel such as site managers, shift managers and book-room staff review and approve records for overtime payment processing via PSP.
|4 |Agency Timesheet |Cross-references agency worker shift plans against PDA and Console attendance scan data. Approved records are transmitted to the JoinedUp agency payroll platform via API, eliminating manual data re-entry and accelerating cost recognition.
|5 |RCS Reporting |Reconciles planned versus actual hours across delivery offices and processing sites, disaggregated by workforce category - AWD, overtime, and agency. Generates structured data sets consumed by Royal Mail's financial reporting infrastructure for cost governance and expenditure management.
|===

*Top-level functional requirements (Must-have, MVP):*

* Import Processing duty data from MDS into [Enter Solution/Application Acronym here, e.g PDA] for all Mail Centres, Parcel Hubs and Super Hubs (MDS++_++MVP01++_++01).
* Allocate duty holders and rotation weeks against the imported duty data (MDS++_++MVP03++_++01–03).
* Generate, edit and view daily attendance plans for a four-weeks-past / four-weeks-future window (MDS++_++MVP04++_++01–08).
* Bring in and manage overtime duties from MDS, with auto-purge after four weeks (MDS++_++MVP19++_++01–03).
* Manually create and amend overtime duties locally (MDS++_++MVP05++_++01–02).
* Approve overtime against PDA / Console scan data and transmit authorised records to PSP (MDS++_++MVP10++_++01–05).
* Approve schedule attendance against scan data and transmit to PSP (MDS++_++MVP11++_++01–05).
* Provide Production Schedulers with 10-minute-granularity work-area headcount reports (MDS++_++MVP06++_++01).
* Provide WAM/PSM with Employee Line View and Work Area Line View, including print outputs (MDS++_++MVP07, MDS++_++MVP08).
* Merge the existing Deliveries and Processing SISO applications to remove the cross-access technical limitation (MDS++_++MVP16++_++01).
* Send reporting feeds (attendance plan, OT, headcount) from [Enter Solution/Application Acronym here, e.g PDA] to DP/Qlik and back to MDS (MDS++_++MVP14++_++01, MDS++_++MVP15++_++01).

*Out-of-scope for MVP (recorded for traceability):*

* Future Duty Planning of differing duty structures (MDS++_++MVP13++_++01 - Won't-have; duty data revisions are infrequent and master data refreshes monthly).
* Live Scan reporting and Pay-vs-Scan reporting in DP (MDS++_++MVP22, MDS++_++MVP23 - deferred).
* PSP integration for future-dated employee records (out of scope for MVP).
