== Cross-cutting Concepts

*Content*

_This section describes crosscutting concepts (practices, patterns,
regulations or solution ideas). Such concepts are often related to
multiple building blocks._

*Motivation*

_Concepts form the basis for *conceptual integrity* (consistency,
homogeneity) of the architecture. Thus, they are an important
contribution to achieve inner qualities of your system._

_This is the place in the template that we provided for a cohesive
specification of such concepts._

_Many of these concepts relate to or influence several of your building
blocks._

*Form*

_The form can be varied:_

* concept papers with any kind of structure
* example implementations,especially for technical concepts
* cross-cutting model excerpts or scenarios using notations of the
architecture views

*Royal Mail FDS - Processing Sites: Worked Example*

=== Identity, Authentication and Authorisation

Authentication is delegated to Royal Mail AD via SAML; authorisation is enforced through AD groups including Planner-++<++of Location++>++, Approver-++<++of Location++>++, Book-room-++<++of Location++>++.

=== Audit and Traceability

An append-only audit store retains every state-changing action: plan amendments, OT/SA approvals, agency-timesheet submissions, and manual overrides. Each record carries actor identity, timestamp, source data references (scan event IDs, plan IDs). Retention is seven years, aligned with audit requirements.

=== Integration Resilience

All outbound integrations are idempotent and replayable. PSP and JoinedUp submissions use opaque idempotency keys derived from (employee, shift, approval-version).Dead-letter queues are monitored with alert thresholds.

=== Data Privacy and Retention

Personal data minimisation: only employee identifiers required for plan/scan/pay reconciliation are processed; names and full personal records remain in source systems. Retention schedules align with the RMG data-protection policy (7 years of archived data, 1 year of live data). Subject-access requests are serviceable through a support request.

=== Logging, Monitoring and Observability

Structured JSON logs, application metrics and distributed traces are emitted from every component and forwarded to the central observability platform. Service-level objectives (SLOs) for each capability are published; alerting is keyed off SLO burn rates rather than raw error counts. Operational dashboards expose plan-vs-scan reconciliation health, payroll backlog, and integration error rates.

=== User-experience consistency across the merged web app shell

The merger of the Deliveries and Processing shells (MDS++_++MVP16++_++01) is designed to be invisible to users: a single navigation, a single notification model, and a single search. Per-capability screens follow the existing Delivery patterns to minimise re-training cost for users moving between domains.
